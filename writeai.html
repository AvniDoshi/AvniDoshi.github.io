WRITE AI Center

A complete digital presence for a national research center studying how generative AI can improve writing instruction in postsecondary education.

Visit WRITE AI Center

Overview

WRITE AI brings together researchers, educators, institutional partners, and technology developers to build evidence for effective, responsible, and equitable uses of generative AI in writing education.

The website serves as the Center's public home, connecting its research, PapyrusAI platform, training and events, resources, news, people, and partner organizations in one cohesive experience.

What I did

I designed and built the entire working website from end to end. I established the visual direction, information architecture, responsive layouts, typography, color system, navigation, reusable components, and interaction patterns across the full site.

I translated the Center's research mission and extensive institutional content into clear pathways for different audiences. I created flexible page systems for research areas, resources, training, news, people, and partner profiles, then implemented and refined each experience for accessibility, consistency, and ease of use across desktop and mobile.

I also designed the interactive AI tools directory, giving visitors a structured way to search and compare tools by writing stage while clearly communicating cost, privacy, and available evidence.

A unified research platform

The design balances the credibility of a national research center with a welcoming editorial voice. A consistent visual system connects complex research questions, PapyrusAI, public resources, and organizational stories without flattening the purpose of each section.
